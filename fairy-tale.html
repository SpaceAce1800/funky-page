Synopsis

Fairy Tale is Stephen King's enchanting foray into the realm of classic fantasy adventure. The story follows Charlie Reade, a down-on-his-luck high school student who befriends his elderly neighbor, Howard Bowitch. When Howard mysteriously passes away, Charlie discovers a hidden door in his basement—a portal to a magical realm known as Empis.

In this fantastical world, Charlie must navigate a dangerous landscape filled with bizarre creatures, dark secrets, and ancient powers. What begins as an escape from his troubled life becomes an epic quest where Charlie must face his greatest fears and discover his true strength. As the line between reality and magic blurs, Charlie finds himself fighting not just for his own survival, but for the fate of both worlds.

King masterfully weaves themes of redemption, courage, and the power of literature itself into this thrilling adventure that celebrates the magic of storytelling and the hero that exists within us all.
